STEP RIGHT UP!

First, hold to pour the coffee and let go at the green line. Then pour the steamed milk up to the next line. Too much and it overflows! Pick your topping, an extra and your mug. Then carry it across the snowy circus to your friend at the Ferris wheel. Wind, snow bumps and icy patches make it wobble, so keep it level and don't spill.

How wild

★ WINTER CARNIVAL · SNOWBALL SPLAT ★

STEP RIGHT UP!

Eighteen snowballs. Snowmen, little aliens and polar cubs pop up from behind the snow banks. Tap one to lob a snowball. The snowmen up front are worth 1, the aliens 2 and the tiny polar cubs at the back 3. Penguins are friends, so splatting one costs 2. Catch the golden ornament floating by for 5.

How wild
Touch or mouse: tap where you want the snowball to land. It takes a moment to fly, so aim for ones that just popped up.
Keyboard: arrow keys to aim, space to throw.
Prizes: holiday prizes! 12 points for a small one, 22 for a big one, 32 for a JUMBO.

good to know

Which size do I upload?

Start with the 64px PNG. If it looks too big on your page, use the 32px one, which is close to a normal cursor's size. The 128px file is the full-detail version, for uploaders that shrink images themselves.

The click lands in the wrong spot.

Most cursor uploaders click from the image's top-left corner, which is why the prompt asks for the tip or a sparkle there. If clicks feel off, tell the AI where they land and ask it to move the tip or the sparkle to match.

My download came out as a .txt file.

Some chat apps mislabel image files when you download them. Rename the file so it ends in .png and it opens normally. Or open the image, right-click it and choose Save image.

How do I use it on my own website?

Put the 32px file next to your page and add this to your CSS. The two numbers are the click point, in pixels from the top-left. The AI gives you these if you tick "My own website" in step 1.

body { cursor: url("my-cursor-32.png") 2 2, auto; }

Keep auto at the end: it's the fallback if the image doesn't load. Browsers may ignore cursor images larger than 32px.

Is anything sent to Magick Mica or anyone else?

No. The prompt is built on this page, in your browser. Your answers are remembered on this device only, so they're still here if you come back. "Start over" clears them.

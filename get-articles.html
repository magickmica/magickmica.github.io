good to know

Is anything sent to Magick Mica or anyone else?

No. The collector talks only to your own newsletter, from your browser, and saves the file to your computer. Merging and exporting happen on this page, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How does the second time work?

Keep the JSON from step 4. Next time, choose I have a file and drop it in. We find your newest post and make a collector that stops a few days before it. Then drop the new file in step 3 and save the combined JSON again. Full text you saved before is kept.

Should I tick "full text"?

Tick it if you want a real backup of your writing: your archive page will then let you read every post right there, even offline. Leave it off for a quick list of titles, dates and links.

Can I use Substack's own export zip?

Yes. In Substack go to Settings → Exports → Create new export, download the zip, and drop it in step 3 as it is. We read it right here in your browser: the post list and each post's full text, with a cover picked from the post's own images. We only open posts.csv and the posts folder. Your subscriber list, which is also in that zip, is never read.

Export or collector: which should I use?

The export has everything, including email-only posts, but you have to ask Substack for a new one each time. The collector is quicker for topping up, and adds hearts and comment counts. They mix: drop an export and a collector file together and each post is kept once.

It says it can't find the newsletter.

Run it on your newsletter's own address, yourname.substack.com or your custom domain, not on substack.com itself or inside the Substack app.

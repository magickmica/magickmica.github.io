The four bands

50–100Low risk
100–200Moderate risk
200–300High risk
300+Extreme risk

The dollar index tracks the US dollar against a basket of major currencies. The 10-year yield is what the US government pays to borrow for ten years. When both climb together, financial conditions tighten, and the MMRI rises.

Fireside Cocoa

It's snowing outside and you're a fresh mug of hot cocoa. Slide around the cabin and sip up every coffee bean and marshmallow before the cold gets you. Reach a crackling fire and for a few seconds the cold things melt and run — catch them then. Arrows, WASD, swipe, or the pad below.

🎨 THREE CABINS — PICK ONE ABOVE THE MAZE, OR PRESS T
📜 HOW TO PLAY

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to slide · T switches theme · P or space to pause · Esc leaves full screen
Touch: swipe across the maze, or use the pad

Trump & Lincoln: The Golden Tub Talk

Trump: Look at this tub. Tremendous tub. Possibly the best bath anyone's ever seen. People are talking about it.

Lincoln: Sir… I have endured war, debate, and the burden of a divided nation. I did not expect my greatest trial to be… a glittering soup.

Trump: Not soup—luxury. This is what winning looks like. You step in, you come out refreshed. Reborn. Maybe even more popular.

Lincoln: I find that character is not forged in comfort… nor in bubbles that shimmer like a disco sunrise.

Trump & Lincoln: The Gold Toilet Debate

Trump: Abe, listen to me. History remembers luxury. Nobody remembers average bathrooms.

Lincoln: I freed the nation, sir. I did not gild the plumbing.

Trump: Exactly the problem. Tremendous oversight. Imagine Gettysburg… but with gold toilets. Powerful. Inspirational.

Lincoln: I do not believe porcelain determines liberty.

Trump: Wrong. Completely wrong. A shiny restroom says civilization. Rome understood this. Versailles understood this. Even the pharaohs probably had unbelievable bathrooms.

Lincoln: The Union was preserved without crystal faucets.

Trump: But could it have been preserved better with crystal faucets? Think bigger, Abe. Bigger. More sparkle.

Lincoln: Sir, I fear the people may prefer bread over gilded commodes.

Trump: You'd be surprised. The people love glamour. Gold says success. Gold says destiny. Gold says “this nation moisturizes.”

Lincoln: I intended this republic to stand upon principles.

Trump: And marble floors. Holographic ceilings. Maybe a chandelier above the bathtub. We can do both.

Lincoln: I wore the same hat for years.

Trump: And it worked! Branding! Iconic silhouette. Now imagine that hat reflected in twenty-four karat toilet water. Presidential.

Lincoln: That is perhaps the strangest sentence ever spoken in this house.

Trump: No, the strangest sentence is “Let's make the bathroom smaller.” Nobody says that.

Lincoln: The nation suffers grave division and you discuss reflective plumbing.

Trump: Exactly why we need morale. Bubble baths. Gold fixtures. Cosmic lighting. National healing through interior design.

Lincoln: You propose unity… through luxurious restroom ambiance?

Trump: Finally, he understands me.

Lincoln: I confess… the marble does shimmer rather beautifully at sunset.

Trump: That's the spirit, Abe. We start with one gold toilet. Then maybe a fountain. Then perhaps… a glitter ceiling.

Lincoln: I shall permit exactly one chandelier.

Trump: Tremendous victory for America.

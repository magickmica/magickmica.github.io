The Ghost Who Shared Coffee With the Moon Baby

a bedtime story for grown dreamers

Every evening, just before the first stars remembered to sparkle, a little ghost wandered into the Wildflower Meadow Between Worlds carrying the same tiny mug of stardust coffee.

No one knew where the ghost came from. Some said they drifted out of forgotten dreams. Some whispered they were born from the very first sigh the universe ever made.

Because every night, the Moon Baby would rise. Not the moon everyone else saw — the little moon who lived inside the moon, who painted silver onto oceans and sprinkled glow onto mushrooms before dawn.

“What does coffee taste like?” they asked one evening. The ghost held out the mug. “It tastes like staying up just long enough to hear the flowers tell secrets.”

The Moon Baby took one tiny sip. “It tastes like… remembering tomorrow before it happens.”

“Sometimes they watched comets practise their handwriting across the sky.”
“Sometimes they counted planets the way children count sheep.”
“Galaxies hummed lullabies so old that even time forgot the words.”

“Somewhere beyond ordinary dreams, friendship was still being brewed — one cup of stardust coffee at a time.”

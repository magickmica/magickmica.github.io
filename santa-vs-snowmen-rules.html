Santa vs. the Snowmen

How to play

The snowmen have come to life, and they want Santa. Dash through the snowbanks and gather every present before they catch you.

✦ PLAY SANTA VS. THE SNOWMEN

Controls

  • Keyboard — arrows or WASD to dash · T switches theme · P or space to pause · Esc leaves full screen.
  • Touch — swipe across the maze, or use the pad under it.
  • Themes — three skies to play under: North Pole, Aurora and Sugarplum. Pick one above the maze, tap 🎨 THEME on the scoreboard, or press T.

The four snowmen

Big Chill
Black top hat, red scarf. Comes straight for you and never gives up.
Sleet
Blue beanie. Doesn't chase you — slides to where you're about to be.
Flurry
Green bobble hat. Blows about at random, then drifts your way when you least expect it.
Slush
Purple earmuffs. Guards his own corner of the maze. Come too close and he rolls after you.

How the night works

  • Present — 10 points. Gather every one to end the night and start the next.
  • Hot cocoa — 50 points, and the snowmen start to melt and run. Catch them for 200, 400, 800, 1600.
  • Four tunnels — the gaps at the top, the bottom and both sides come out the opposite edge. The snowmen move slowly through them; you don't.
  • The snow fort — the yard in the middle. The snowmen start there and roll back there when you catch them. You can't get in.
  • Starlight — the maze starts dark and your glow grows as you gather. Turn it off with the Starlight button under the maze if you'd rather see everything.
  • Full screen — the ⛶ button in the top corner of the maze, or the one on the start screen; ✕ or Esc to come back. On a phone in landscape the pad moves beside the board so the maze gets the whole height.
  • Nice / naughty list — the button under the maze. Nice gives you slower snowmen, longer cocoas and a spare Santa. Speeds change as soon as you press it; the spare Santa arrives with your next game.
  • An extra Santa at 10,000 points. Each new night, everyone moves faster and the cocoa wears off sooner.

The Witching Hour

Drift the web lattice and swallow every candle flame. Four things hunt you. Swallow a full moon and for a few seconds they flee instead — catch them then. Arrows, WASD, swipe, or the pad below.

◂ FOUR TUNNELS · TOP, BOTTOM AND BOTH SIDES ▸

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to drift · P or space to pause · Esc leaves full screen
Touch: swipe across the maze, or use the pad

The four that hunt you

🧙The Witch
Comes straight for you. No tricks, no mercy, never lets go.
👹The Goblin
Doesn't chase you — chases where you're about to be. Cuts you off.
🦇The Bat
Half mad. Mostly random, but it drifts your way often enough to matter.
🕷️The Spider
Minds its own corner of the web until you come close. Then it pounces.

How the hour works

  • Candle flame — 10 points. Clear every one to end the hour and start the next.
  • Full moon — 50 points, and the four turn pale and run. Catch them for 200, 400, 800, 1600.
  • Four tunnels — the gaps at the top, the bottom and both sides come out the opposite edge. The four move slowly through them; you don't.
  • The cauldron — the chamber in the middle. They start there and they return there when you catch them. You can't get in.
  • Candlelight — the maze starts dark and lights as you eat. Turn it off above if you'd rather see everything.
  • Full screen — the button above, or the ✕ in the corner to come back. On a phone it fills the screen even where the browser has no real full-screen mode, and in landscape the pad moves beside the board so the maze gets the whole height.
  • Gentle haunt / full moon — the button above. Gentle gives you a slower four, longer moons and a spare ghost. Speeds change as soon as you press it; the spare ghost arrives with your next game.
  • An extra ghost at 10,000 points. Each new hour, everyone moves faster and the moons last less long.

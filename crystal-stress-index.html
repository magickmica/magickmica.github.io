    The pattern held in both halves of the record, 2000–2012 and 2013–2026. It is a gauge of how rough the road is, not a crash timer: it often reads high while trouble is already under way, and some of the best buying chances came in Storm. For fun and education only. Not financial advice.

    How it's made

    1. Fear: the VIX level. Weight 2.
    2. Credit & conditions: the Chicago Fed's National Financial Conditions Index. Weight 2.
    3. Dollar surge: how much the dollar index moved over the last three months. Weight 1.
    4. Rate shock: how much the 10-year yield moved over the last three months. Weight 1.
    5. Energy: the price of WTI crude oil. Weight 1.
    6. Curve: the 10-year yield minus the 2-year yield. Weight 1.
    7. Safe haven: the price of gold. Weight ½.

    Each pillar is ranked against its own past 252 trading days, so every ingredient speaks on the same 0–100 scale no matter its units. The index is the weighted average of the seven ranks. Data from Yahoo Finance and the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), refreshed about every ten minutes.

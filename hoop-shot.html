STEP RIGHT UP!

Forty seconds. Swipe up to shoot. Aim with the direction of your swipe, and how fast you swipe decides how hard you throw. Every basket is 2 points, or 3 once you are on fire with three in a row. On Normal and Wild the hoop starts to slide after ten seconds.

How wild
Touch or mouse: swipe upward toward the hoop. A steady, medium-fast swipe works best.
Keyboard: ← / → to aim, hold space and let go when the bar is in the glowing zone.
Prizes: 16 points for a small prize, 30 for a big one, 44 for a JUMBO.

SNOWMAN BUILDER

Build a snowman. Dress him up. Don't let him melt!

★ WINTER CARNIVAL · SNOWMAN BUILDER ★

STEP RIGHT UP!

First, hold to roll each snowball and let go when it lands in the green. Too small and he looks wobbly, too big and it crumbles. Then pick his hat, nose and scarf. Last, the sun comes out! Drag the cloud over the sun and tap falling snowflakes to keep him frosty for 45 seconds.

How wild
Touch or mouse: hold to roll, drag the cloud, tap snowflakes.
Keyboard: hold space to roll, ← / → move the cloud, space grabs a snowflake.
Prizes: holiday prizes! 50 points for a small one, 75 for a big one, 95 for a JUMBO.

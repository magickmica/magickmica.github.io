Imagine not just a like button but a eye button that would list all the people that looked.♥ 5↻ 0

Imagine not just a like button but a eye button that would list all the people that looked. Creepy or the next substack surveillance move? So I asked my Chat GTP …. 😂 Honestly? Both. 😂 As a feature, an 👁️ “who viewed this” button would be wildly compelling. It would turn passive readership into visible social behavior. But the second you can see exactly who looked, the psychological contract changes. Right now: Like = “I publicly acknowledge this.”View = “I privately consumed this.” An eye button would basically say: “You thought you were lurking anonymously? Absolutely not.” And THAT is where the creepiness begins. I could also see the very Substack-y version being: 👁 47 people viewed this - Want to know who? Upgrade to Substack Pro™. 😂 But there's an interesting middle ground: show the number of views, but not the identities. That gives writers useful feedback without turning every post into a tiny surveillance dashboard. Or, if they really wanted to embrace chaos: 👁 Who's Watching 12 subscribers 4 non-subscribers 3 people who absolutely refuse to like anything 1 person who has read this 7 times 1 person you blocked in 2024 👀 Now THAT would be the next Substack surveillance move.

Graveyard Shift

How to play

You are the jack-o'-lantern. Roll through the graveyard and gobble every piece of candy corn while four things climb out of the crypt to hunt you.

✦ PLAY GRAVEYARD SHIFT

Controls

  • Keyboard — arrows or WASD to roll · P or space to pause · Esc leaves full screen.
  • Touch — swipe across the maze, or use the pad under it.

The four that hunt you

🧛The Vampire
Smells you from across the yard and comes straight at you. Never tires.
🐺The Werewolf
Hunts like a wolf — runs for where you're headed, not where you are.
🧟The Zombie
Shambles about with no plan at all, then lurches your way when you least expect it.
💀The Skeleton
Guards its own corner of the graveyard. Wander too close and it rattles after you.

How the night works

  • Candy corn — 10 points. Clear every piece to end the night and start the next.
  • Witch's brew — 50 points, and the four turn pale and run. Catch them for 200, 400, 800, 1600.
  • Four tunnels — the gaps at the top, the bottom and both sides come out the opposite edge. The four move slowly through them; you don't.
  • The crypt — the vault in the middle. They start there and they crawl back there when you catch them. You can't get in.
  • Lantern light — the graveyard starts dark and your glow grows as you eat. Turn it off with the Lantern Light button under the maze if you'd rather see everything.
  • Full screen — the ⛶ button in the top corner of the maze, or the one on the start screen; ✕ or Esc to come back. On a phone it fills the screen even where the browser has no real full-screen mode, and in landscape the pad moves beside the board so the maze gets the whole height.
  • Treat / trick — the button under the maze. Treat gives you a slower four, longer brews and a spare pumpkin. Speeds change as soon as you press it; the spare pumpkin arrives with your next game.
  • An extra pumpkin at 10,000 points. Each new night, everyone moves faster and the brews wear off sooner.

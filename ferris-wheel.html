COSMIC FERRIS WHEEL

Ride up into the night sky. Wish on shooting stars.

★ RIDE TO THE TOP OF THE SKY ★

STEP RIGHT UP!

Climb into the gold gondola and ride the Cosmic Ferris Wheel up into the night sky. It takes about a minute. Tap shooting stars as they streak by to make a wish. At the very top the wheel stops for a moment, and a note from Magick Mica floats by. Your first ride each day pays 2 tickets.

Touch or mouse: tap a shooting star.
Keyboard: space wishes on the star that is flying.
This one is a ride, not a race. Sit back and enjoy the view.

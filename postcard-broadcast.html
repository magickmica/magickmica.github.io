Celestial Gazette declares: “She's the main character”

By Luna Quillsong · Senior Moon Correspondent

Residents across the Kingdom of Somewhere Else awoke yesterday to discover the moon had abandoned its usual silver complexion in favour of a spectacular shade of cosmic rose. Astronomers called it impossible. Witches called it Tuesday.

According to the Department of Celestial Drama, the Moon entered what officials described as “an unapologetically expressive phase” after overhearing one local starseed remark, “I wish the sky dressed with more confidence.” Within moments, the lunar body reportedly blushed across the heavens.

“It's not a scientific phenomenon. It's emotional lighting.” — Professor Nebula Fern

The event has inspired thousands to wear pink cloaks after sunset, compliment strangers' auras, and dramatically stare into oceans while pretending not to notice they're being admired. Officials expect the Moon to remain fabulous indefinitely.

✨ Fairy Dust Shortage Causes Chaos in Town

Pixies union demands better working conditions

By Petal Bramble · Labor & Magic Reporter

The Great Fairy Dust Strike entered its seventh day this morning after pixies unanimously voted to suspend all sparkle-related activities until safer working conditions are established. The strike has already affected enchanted forests, birthday wishes, dandelion flights, and approximately 94% of accidental magic worldwide.

“We've been sprinkling glitter into people's lives for centuries. And frankly… we're exhausted.” — Glimmia Twinkletoes, union leader

The union's demands include:

  • Longer moonbeam breaks
  • Unlimited flower nectar
  • Paid rainbow holidays
  • Hazard pay for unicorn grooming
  • A complete ban on glitter inhalation

Without fresh fairy dust, enchanted mirrors have become brutally honest, pumpkins refuse to become carriages, and at least six dragons have forgotten how to sparkle. Negotiations continue under a large toadstool.

🧙 Wizards Ban Boring People From the Castle

Broomstick passes revoked in mass enchantment cleanse

By Cedric Spellwright

“Magic cannot thrive where enthusiasm goes to die.” — Archmage Bellweather

Castle security has been equipped with new Wonder Detectors, enchanted devices capable of measuring a person's curiosity levels. Visitors may now be asked: “When was the last time you danced with absolutely no reason?” Failure to answer may result in temporary transformation into decorative garden statuary. Tourism has increased 400%.

“It's not just a phase…”
